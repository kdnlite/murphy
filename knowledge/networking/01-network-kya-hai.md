# Network kya hota hai

Network do ya zyada devices (computer, phone, printer, server) ka aapas mein
juda hona hai taaki ve data aadaan-pradaan kar sakein. Jab aapka phone aur
laptop same WiFi par hote hain, to ve ek local network (LAN) ka hissa hote hain.

## Network ke types
- LAN (Local Area Network): ek ghar, office ya building ke andar ka network. WiFi
  ya LAN cable se banta hai.
- WAN (Wide Area Network): door-door ke networks ko jodta hai. Internet duniya ka
  sabse bada WAN hai.
- WLAN: wireless LAN, yaani WiFi se bana LAN.

## Zaroori cheezein
- Node: network se juda koi bhi device.
- Client: woh device jo service maangta hai (jaise aapka browser).
- Server: woh device jo service deta hai (jaise website ka server).
- Bandwidth: ek samay mein kitna data ja sakta hai; "pipe ki motai" samajh lo.
- Latency: data ko ek jagah se doosri jagah pahunchne mein laga samay (ping).

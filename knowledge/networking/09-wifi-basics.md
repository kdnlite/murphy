# WiFi ki buniyaad

WiFi radio waves se bina taar ke network deta hai. Aapka device aur router hawa
mein signal ke zariye data bhejte hain.

## Bands
- 2.4 GHz: door tak jata hai, deewar paar karta hai, par dheema aur bheedh-bhaad
  wala.
- 5 GHz: tez, par kam door tak.
- 6 GHz (WiFi 6E): aur tez, sabse kam bheedh.

## SSID aur channels
- SSID: WiFi ka naam jo aap dekhte ho.
- Channel: band ke andar ka ek rasta; paas ke routers alag channel par ho to kam
  takraav.

## Signal
- Door jaane par, deewaron se, aur doosre devices se signal kamzor hota hai.
- Router ko khule, ooncha aur beech mein rakhna behtar hota hai.

## Standards
WiFi 4 (n), WiFi 5 (ac), WiFi 6 (ax), WiFi 7 — naye utne tez aur behtar.
